ClinIQ

Hackathon prototype

ClinIQ demonstrates a multi-agent reasoning architecture designed to reduce diagnostic anchoring by independently examining contradictions, cognitive bias, safety risks, alternative hypotheses and evidence.

Important: This is a simulated software demonstration. The clinical cases, reasoning outputs and evidence references are synthetic and must not be used to make real clinical decisions.
